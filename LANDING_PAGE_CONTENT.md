```
Frequently Asked Questions
```

### FAQ Items

**Q1: Is 9jastock a stockbroker? Can I buy stocks through you?**
```
No, 9jastock is not a stockbroker. We are an investment intelligence
platform that helps you analyze, track, and make better decisions.
To buy stocks, you'll still need a licensed stockbroker. We're
working on broker integrations for the future.
```

**Q2: How accurate is the stock data?**
```
Our data comes from TradingView and Yahoo Finance - the same sources
used by professional traders worldwide. Premium users get 5-minute
updates, Pro users get 1-minute real-time data.
```

**Q3: What happens after my 7-day trial?**
```
You'll be charged for your selected plan (monthly or annual). You can
cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
```

**Q4: Can I track stocks from other countries?**
```
Not yet - but it's coming! We're building global market support
(US, UK, crypto) which will launch in the coming months. Subscribe
now and you'll get it when it's ready at no extra cost.
```

**Q5: Is my data safe?**
```
Yes. We use bank-level encryption, never share your data with third
parties, and comply with Nigerian Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).
```

**Q6: What payment methods do you accept?**
```
We accept all Nigerian bank cards, bank transfers via Paystack,
and international cards via Stripe.
```

**Q7: Can I get a refund?**
```
If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days of your paid
subscription, contact us for a full refund. No questions asked.
```

**Q8: Is this financial advice?**
```
No. 9jastock provides information and tools for educational purposes.
Always do your own research and consult a licensed financial advisor
before making investment decisions.
```

## Design Notes

### Colors (Suggested)
- Primary: Deep green (Nigerian flag inspired) #008751
- Secondary: White #FFFFFF
- Accent: Gold #FCD116
- Text: Dark gray #1A1A1A
- Background: Light gray #F5F5F5

### Typography
- Headlines: Bold, modern sans-serif (Inter, Poppins)
- Body: Clean, readable (Inter, Open Sans)
- Accent: Slightly rounded for friendly feel

### Imagery Style
- Real app screenshots (not mockups)
- Nigerian-themed (Naira symbol, local stock names visible)
- Diverse representation in any human imagery
- Professional but approachable

### Mobile Considerations
- Hero image should stack below text on mobile
- Feature cards should be single column
- Pricing cards should stack vertically
- FAQ should be accordion style
- Sticky CTA button at bottom on mobile

### Animations (Subtle)
- Fade in sections on scroll
- Counter animation for stats
- Hover effects on cards
- Smooth accordion for FAQ
